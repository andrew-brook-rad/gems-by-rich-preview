Step 03 / The finer points

Decide which differences matter.

You can leave any grade open. Rich can help you compare the appearance behind the report.

Colour How little body colour is present

D–F is colourless; G–J is near-colourless. The difference you notice depends on the stone, lighting and setting.

Clarity Inclusions seen during grading

Higher grades describe greater rarity. VS and SI stones are worth comparing, but no grade guarantees an eye-clean appearance. Ask about visible inclusions, transparency and durability.

What do the letters mean?

FL: Flawless. IF: Internally Flawless. VVS: Very Very Slightly Included. VS: Very Slightly Included. SI: Slightly Included. The 1 and 2 subdivisions distinguish levels within those categories. Clarity is assessed at 10× magnification.

This filter applies to round examples only. Other shapes remain eligible: their polish and symmetry do not replace an overall assessment of light performance.

More details Fluorescence, reports & proportions

These are optional. There is no single table, depth or ratio range that makes every shape beautiful.

Fluorescence is a response to UV, which is also in sunlight. Judge its effect on the individual stone. Sample lab labels demonstrate filtering; there are no real reports attached.
