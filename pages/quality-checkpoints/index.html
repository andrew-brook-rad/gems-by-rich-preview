10 Our Ten Quality Checkpoints

Annotated ring sketch, numbered: artwork to follow
Front elevation sketch: artwork to follow
Top view: artwork to follow
Side profile: artwork to follow

  1. The signature shank is subtly squared, so the ring does not twist or turn on the finger.

  2. Gold is alloyed nickel-free and palladium-rich, so white metal stays white for years.

  3. Settings are burnished: denser, less porous metal with a more substantial feel.

  4. Every prong is made by hand, which holds a stone more securely than a cast one.

  5. Even the petite pavé prongs are cut individually, so nothing catches on fabric.

  6. The bench skills behind each piece are handed down, not learned from a machine.

  7. Diamonds are matched by eye for colour and sparkle before they are ever set.

  8. Six polishing stages bring maximum brilliance out of the metal and the stone.

  9. Solder seams are cleaned and re-polished so no joint is visible under a loupe.

  10. The finished ring is re-measured, weighed and photographed before it is handed over.
