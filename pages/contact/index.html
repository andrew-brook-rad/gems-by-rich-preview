Common questions

Do I need to know exactly what I want?+

Not at all. A favourite colour, an occasion or even a feeling is a lovely place to start. Rich can help you explore the possibilities.

Can I buy a gem directly on the website?+

Every enquiry starts with a conversation. Rich will discuss the stone, confirm its availability and talk you through the next steps before you decide.

Can I start with a budget?+

Of course. Share a range you feel comfortable with, alongside the colours, shapes or designs you love. That gives Rich a useful starting point for your search.

Can I bring my own ideas for a piece?+

Yes. A sketch, a reference image or a story behind the occasion can help start the design conversation. Rich will explore the possibilities with you.
