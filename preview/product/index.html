Personalise / Customise details

Customising opens the design studio: stone, shape, carat, metal and size are chosen there with Rich.

Details

Made to order around your stone in 18k yellow, white or rose gold, or platinum 950. Shown with an oval; also made for round, pear, cushion and emerald cuts. About six weeks from the day the stone is chosen.

Sizing guide

Malaysian, Hong Kong, US and UK sizes all accepted. Rich confirms against a physical ring gauge before making. Resizing within the first year is free on plain and knife-edge bands.

Care and guidance

Rinse in warm water with a drop of dish soap and a soft brush; avoid ultrasonic cleaners on emeralds and treated stones. Remove before the gym, the beach and household chemicals. Complimentary polish and claw check every twelve months.

Shipping information

Hand-delivered in Kuala Lumpur by appointment, or shipped fully insured worldwide with tracking and signature. Duties and local taxes are the recipient's responsibility. Every piece travels with its GIA report and a written appraisal for insurance.
